Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Atlas Set Travel respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using this website.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect details you provide such as your name, email address, phone number, destination interests, travel dates, number of travelers, budget, and any message content you share.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide travel-planning support, and communicate about your requested services. We may also use aggregated website activity data to improve site performance and user experience.

Analytics

This website uses analytics tools to understand traffic and engagement trends. Analytics data may include technical details such as browser type, approximate location, and pages visited.

Form Processing

Inquiry submissions are processed through a third-party form delivery service. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal or financial information through the website form.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only with service providers necessary to operate the site and respond to your inquiry, or when required by law.

Your Choices

You may request updates or deletion of your inquiry information by contacting us at helloatlasset@gmail.com.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for information submitted through this website, but no internet transmission method is guaranteed to be fully secure.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Atlas Set Travel at helloatlasset@gmail.com or 786-503-5312.
